Cleaning jobs in Charlotte

Part time W2 positions with clear pay, a clear checklist and a team that backs you up.

What to expect

  • W2 employee, with payroll taxes handled
  • Starting pay $18 an hour, paid for all working hours including drive time between homes
  • A bonus on every job done to standard and on time. Most cleans work out to about $23 to $35 an hour
  • You keep 100% of tips
  • Mileage between homes paid at the IRS rate
  • Daytime jobs that start between 8 AM and 1 PM, on the days you choose
  • Supplies, equipment and paid training provided
  • Laundry of cloths and mop pads is handled for you

Pay details are confirmed in your offer letter.

What we look for

  • At least 1 year of paid cleaning experience: a cleaning company, private clients or hotel housekeeping
  • A reliable car and a valid driver's license
  • Able to pass a background check. A past record does not automatically rule you out
  • On time, detail oriented and good at keeping in touch

Part time to start, growing as we book more homes. No resume needed.
