What's included

This is the checklist our cleaners follow, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

Every regular visit

The same standard every time.

Kitchen

  • Counters and backsplash wiped
  • Sink and faucet cleaned
  • Stovetop cleaned
  • Microwave wiped inside and out
  • Appliance fronts wiped
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Toilet cleaned inside and out
  • Sink, counter and mirror cleaned
  • Tub and shower cleaned
  • Fixtures polished
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash emptied

Living areas and bedrooms

  • Surfaces dusted
  • Floors vacuumed, hard floors mopped
  • Beds made when linens are left out
  • Trash emptied
  • Mirrors and glass spot cleaned

The rotation

Each regular visit, one area of your home also gets extra attention, so over a few visits the whole house gets a deeper clean without paying for a deep clean each time.

  • Baseboards and door frames
  • Ceiling fans and vents
  • Blinds and window sills
  • Cabinet fronts and handles
  • Grout and shower doors
  • Under furniture where reachable

First visit reset

Your first visit covers the regular checklist plus a full pass on the rotation items above, so every area starts from the same clean baseline.

Not included

  • Inside the oven, inside the fridge and interior windows (available as add-ons)
  • Exterior windows, walls and ceilings
  • Laundry, dishes beyond a few in the sink, and organizing
  • Biohazards, mold remediation and pest cleanup

Anything you want us to focus on?

Tell us when you book, or leave a note for the team. We will do our best to match it.
