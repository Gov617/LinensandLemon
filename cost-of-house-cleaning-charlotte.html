How much does house cleaning cost in Charlotte?

How cleaning companies price, what changes the price, and what to ask before you book.

Three ways companies price

  • Flat price by home size. You see the number up front. This is how we price.
  • Hourly per cleaner. Some companies charge about $55 per cleaner per hour, often with a minimum of several hours, which can put a visit above $200.
  • Quote after a walkthrough. Useful for large or unusual homes, but you wait for the number.

In our October 2026 look at Charlotte company websites, posted starting prices for a standard clean of a typical home ran from roughly $150 to $220. Prices change, so treat that as a rough guide.

What our prices look like

A regular weekly clean starts at $115 for a studio or one bedroom and $149 for two bedrooms. Every two weeks and monthly cost a little more per visit. Your first visit is a reset that takes longer, so it costs more than a regular visit. See the full pricing table.

What changes the price

  • Home size, bedrooms and bathrooms
  • How often you book: weekly is cheapest per visit, monthly costs more
  • Condition of the home when we start
  • Pets, which add $15 per visit with us
  • Extras such as inside the oven or fridge

Questions to ask about hidden costs

  • Is there a minimum number of hours or a minimum charge?
  • Are supplies and equipment included?
  • Is tax added at checkout?
  • What does the first visit cost compared with later visits?
  • What is the cancellation fee?
