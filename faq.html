Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how we work. Something missing? Ask us.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients give us a door code or leave a key. Tell us how you would like us to get in when you book.

Do you bring supplies and equipment?

Yes. We bring our own supplies and equipment, including commercial backpack vacuums with HEPA filtration. We clean with plant-based products like Dr. Bronner's Sal Suds, Bon Ami and Seventh Generation. If you prefer particular products, let us know.

Are you insured?

Yes. Linens & Lemons is insured and bonded, and every cleaner passes a background check before working in a home. Ask us and we will send proof of insurance.

Who cleans my home?

Our cleaners are W2 employees of Linens & Lemons, not contractors, and they keep 100% of their tips.

What if I am not happy with the clean?

Tell us within 48 hours and we will send someone back to fix it at no charge.

How does cancelling work?

Cancel or reschedule free with 24 hours notice. With less notice there is a flat $40 fee. If your cleaner arrives and cannot get in, we charge 50% of the visit.

Why is the first visit more expensive?

The first visit is a reset that takes about one and a half times as long, so it costs more than a regular visit. The first visit prices are on the pricing page. After that your regular price applies.

Are you good with pet homes?

Yes. Pet homes are a big part of what we do. Our HEPA-filtered vacuums trap fine pet hair and dander instead of blowing it back into the air, and our cleaners take extra time on hair on floors, rugs, stairs and furniture. Let us know about your pets when you book, including any that should stay in a room or crate while we clean.

Do you charge extra for pets?

Pets add $15 per visit, which covers the extra time for hair and floors. For heavy shedding or furniture covered in hair, we can add extra time at about $60 per cleaner hour and quote it before we start.

Can you help if someone in my home has allergies?

Our vacuums use HEPA filtration, which traps fine dust, pollen and dander so less of it ends up back in the air. Tell us about allergies or product sensitivities when you book and we will plan around them.

How do I pay?

By card after each clean. Tips are optional and go entirely to your cleaner.

Do you clean after a move or before a party?

Yes. A clean before a party or guests is a one time reset at the first visit rate. A move in or move out clean is the first visit rate plus inside the fridge, oven and cabinets: $367.50 for a studio or 1 bedroom, $427.50 for 2 bedrooms, $517.50 for 3 bedrooms, and from $645 for 4 or more. Call or email us to book.
