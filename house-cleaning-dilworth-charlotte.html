House cleaning in Dilworth, Charlotte

Regular, one time and move out cleans for Dilworth houses, bungalows and condos, with the price shown before you book.

Dilworth is one of Charlotte's older neighborhoods. Many homes are single-family houses and bungalows from the early 1900s, and there are also condos, townhomes and apartment buildings, especially near the edges of the neighborhood. We clean every home from the same written checklist, and we bring our own supplies and HEPA filter vacuums.

What a clean costs for a typical Dilworth home

We price by home size, so you know the number before we arrive. The homes we expect to see most here:

  • 1 bedroom condo or apartment: $116 weekly, $122 every two weeks, $129 monthly. First visit or one time clean: $155.
  • 2 bed, 2 bath bungalow: $149 weekly, $157 every two weeks, $166 monthly. First visit: $199.
  • 3 bed, 2.5 bath house: $180 weekly, $190 every two weeks, $200 monthly. First visit: $240.

Some Dilworth houses are larger. A 4 bedroom home starts from $270 weekly, from $284 every two weeks and from $301 monthly, with a first visit from $360. Homes over about 3,000 sq ft add $75 to the first visit for each extra 500 sq ft, which is about $45 more per regular visit. Pets add $15 per visit. Everything else is on the pricing page.

Caring for older homes

Older houses often have hardwood floors, built-ins, trim and detail work that collect dust. Each regular visit gives one area extra attention, like baseboards and door frames, ceiling fans and vents, or blinds and window sills, so the whole home gets a deeper clean over a few visits. See the full rotation.

If your home has not been professionally cleaned in the last 3 months, choose the Deep clean option when you book. It adds $65 and extra time to your first visit only.

Getting in and parking

You do not need to be home. Tell us when you book how we get in: a door code, a lockbox or a key you leave for us. For a house, let us know if we can use the driveway or should park on the street. For a condo or apartment, tell us about any building fob, front desk or visitor parking. Please set this up before the day of your clean. If your cleaner arrives and cannot get in, we charge 50% of the visit.

Families and pets

Plenty of Dilworth homes have kids, a dog, a cat or all three. We clean with plant-based products, and our HEPA filter vacuums trap hair and dander instead of blowing it back into the air. Tell us if a pet should stay in a room or crate while we clean, and about any allergies or product sensitivities.

Moving in or out of Dilworth?

A move in or move out clean covers the full reset plus the inside of the fridge, oven and cabinets. It is $385 for a studio or 1 bedroom, $429 for 2 bedrooms, $470 for 3 bedrooms and from $590 for 4 bedrooms or more. Call or email us to book one.
