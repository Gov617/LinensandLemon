House cleaning in NoDa, Charlotte

Regular, one time and move out cleans for NoDa homes, with the price shown before you book.

NoDa has a little of everything: older mill houses and bungalows, newer townhomes, and apartment buildings near the Blue Line. We clean all of them from the same written checklist, and we bring our own supplies and HEPA filter vacuums.

What a clean costs for a typical NoDa home

We price by home size, so you know the number before we arrive. A few common NoDa homes:

  • 1 bedroom apartment: $115 weekly, $122 every two weeks, $128 monthly. First visit or one time clean: $202.50.
  • 2 bed, 2 bath bungalow or townhome: $149 weekly, $158 every two weeks, $166 monthly. First visit: $262.50.
  • 3 bed, 2.5 bath townhome or house: $179 weekly, $189 every two weeks, $199 monthly. First visit: $315.

Pets add $15 per visit. Larger homes and add-ons are on the pricing page.

Apartments and gated buildings

You do not need to be home. Tell us when you book how we get in: a door or gate code, a key with the front desk, or a lockbox. Let us know about parking and any building rules for vendors, and we will plan around them.

Pet homes

A lot of NoDa homes have a dog or a cat. Our HEPA filter vacuums trap hair and dander instead of blowing it back into the air, and we take extra time on floors, rugs, stairs and furniture. Tell us if a pet should stay in a room or crate while we clean.

Moving in or out of NoDa?

Renters move in and out of NoDa all year. A move out or move in clean covers the full reset plus the inside of the fridge, oven and cabinets, starting at $367.50 for a studio or 1 bedroom.

Why NoDa homes book with us

  • Flat prices by home size, posted before you book
  • Insured and bonded, with background checked cleaners
  • W2 employees who keep 100% of their tips
  • Plant-based products, gentle on homes with kids and pets
  • A re-clean at no charge if you tell us within 48 hours that we missed something
