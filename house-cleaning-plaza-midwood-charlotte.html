House cleaning in Plaza Midwood, Charlotte

Regular, one time and move out cleans for Plaza Midwood homes, with the price shown before you book.

Much of Plaza Midwood is older single-family homes, like Craftsman bungalows and cottages from the early 1900s. Mixed in are newer townhomes, infill houses and apartments, many of them along Central Avenue. We clean every one from the same written checklist, and we bring our own supplies and HEPA filter vacuums.

What a clean costs for a typical Plaza Midwood home

We price by home size, so you know the number before we arrive. The homes we expect to see most here:

  • 1 bedroom apartment: $115 weekly, $122 every two weeks, $128 monthly. First visit or one time clean: $202.50.
  • 2 bed, 2 bath bungalow or cottage: $149 weekly, $158 every two weeks, $166 monthly. First visit: $262.50.
  • 3 bed, 2.5 bath house or townhome: $179 weekly, $189 every two weeks, $199 monthly. First visit: $315.

Pets add $15 per visit. Bigger homes and add-ons are on the pricing page.

Older homes and bigger renovations

Older homes have more trim, door frames and window sills to dust. Our rotation gives one area extra attention each visit, like baseboards, blinds or ceiling fans, so the whole house gets a deeper clean over a few visits.

Some Plaza Midwood bungalows have been added on to, and some new infill homes are larger than they look from the street. Our prices cover up to about 1,300 sq ft for a 2 bedroom and 2,000 sq ft for a 3 bedroom. Larger homes add $75 to the first visit for each extra 500 sq ft, which is about $45 more per regular visit. Tell us your square footage when you book and the price will be right from the start.

Getting in and parking

You do not need to be home. A door code, a lockbox or a key works fine. If you live in an apartment building, tell us about the front desk, gate or vendor rules. On streets with tight parking, let us know if we can use your driveway or where it is best to park.

Pet homes

Plenty of Plaza Midwood homes have a dog or a cat. Our HEPA filter vacuums trap hair and dander instead of blowing it back into the air, and we take extra time on floors, rugs, stairs and furniture. Tell us if a pet should stay in a room or crate while we clean.

Moving in or out?

If you rent in Plaza Midwood and your lease is ending, a move out clean covers the full reset plus the inside of the fridge, oven and cabinets. It is $367.50 for a studio or 1 bedroom, $427.50 for 2 bedrooms and $480 for 3 bedrooms. The same clean works when you are moving in.
