House cleaning in South End, Charlotte

Regular, one time and move out cleans for South End apartments, condos and townhomes, with the price shown before you book.

Most homes in South End are in apartment and condo buildings, many of them fairly new, with townhomes mixed in. A lot of it sits close to the Blue Line light rail. We clean every home from the same written checklist, and we bring our own supplies and HEPA filter vacuums, so you do not need to keep anything on hand for us.

What a clean costs for a typical South End home

We price by home size, so you know the number before we arrive. The homes we expect to see most here:

  • Studio or 1 bedroom apartment: $115 weekly, $122 every two weeks, $128 monthly. First visit or one time clean: $202.50.
  • 2 bed, 2 bath apartment or condo: $149 weekly, $158 every two weeks, $166 monthly. First visit: $262.50.
  • 3 bed, 2.5 bath townhome: $179 weekly, $189 every two weeks, $199 monthly. First visit: $315.

Pets add $15 per visit. Larger homes and add-ons are on the pricing page.

Building access and parking

You do not need to be home. Many South End apartment buildings have a concierge, key fobs or a parking garage, so the main thing we need is a clear way in. When you book, tell us:

  • How we get into the building and your unit: a key left with the concierge, a fob, a lockbox or a door code
  • Whether the concierge or front desk needs our name ahead of time, and any vendor rules your building has
  • Where we should park, such as a visitor spot, a garage level or nearby street parking

Please set this up before the day of your clean. If your cleaner arrives and cannot get in, we charge 50% of the visit. You can cancel or reschedule free with 24 hours notice.

Smaller spaces, kept up on a schedule

Apartments and condos do not take long to get messy again. A weekly or every two week clean keeps the kitchen, bathrooms and floors in good shape. Each regular visit also gives one area extra attention, like baseboards, vents, blinds or grout, so the whole home gets a deeper clean over a few visits. See the full rotation.

Dogs and cats welcome

Lots of South End apartments and condos have a dog or a cat. Our HEPA filter vacuums trap hair and dander instead of blowing it back into the air, and we take extra time on floors, rugs and furniture. Tell us if a pet should stay in a room or crate while we clean.

Lease ending in South End?

Many people here rent, so leases end all year. A move out clean covers the full reset plus the inside of the fridge, oven and cabinets. It is $367.50 for a studio or 1 bedroom, $427.50 for 2 bedrooms and $480 for 3 bedrooms. The same clean works when you are moving in. Call or email us to book one.
