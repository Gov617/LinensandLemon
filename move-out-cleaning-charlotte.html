Move out cleaning in Charlotte

A full clean of an empty home, inside the fridge, oven and cabinets included, at a flat price.

Whether you are handing back keys or moving into a new place, an empty home needs more than a regular clean. We do a full reset of every room and clean the parts no one reaches while the home is lived in.

Move out and move in cleaning prices

  • Studio or 1 bedroom: $367.50
  • 2 bedrooms: $427.50
  • 3 bedrooms: $480
  • 4 bedrooms or more: from $645

That is our first visit reset price plus the inside of the fridge, oven and cabinets. Homes in heavy condition may need more time, and we tell you before we start, never after.

What a move out clean covers

  • Everything on our regular checklist: kitchen, bathrooms, dusting and floors
  • Inside the fridge, oven and all cabinets
  • Baseboards, door frames, vents and ceiling fans
  • Blinds and window sills
  • Grout and shower doors
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped once everything is out

Not included: exterior windows, walls and ceilings, mold remediation, and hauling away furniture or trash left behind.

Tips for getting your deposit back

  • Read your lease or move out checklist first. Some landlords list exactly what they inspect.
  • Book the clean for after the movers leave, so we can reach every floor and shelf.
  • Take photos of each room when we are done, before you return the keys.
  • Leave the power and water on until the clean is finished.
  • Tell us about anything your landlord flagged at move in, and we will give it extra attention.
