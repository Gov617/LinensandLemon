How the first visit works

The first visit is a thorough reset, priced at about one and a quarter times a regular visit, and the first visit prices are in the table above. Regular visits after that follow the rates above.

Deep clean. If your home hasn't been professionally cleaned in the last 3 months, choose the Deep clean option when you book. It adds $65 and extra time to your first visit only, for buildup, baseboards and detail work.

If your home needs much more time than you booked, your cleaner sends us photos before starting and we check with you. You can add extra time at about $60 per cleaner hour, or we clean what fits in the booked time. We never surprise you with a charge after.

One time cleans, such as before guests arrive, are priced the same way as a first visit, because they also need a full reset.

Move in and move out cleans are the first visit price plus the Deep clean option and inside the fridge, oven and cabinets, since empty homes need those done: $385 for a studio or 1 bedroom, $429 for 2 bedrooms, $470 for 3 bedrooms, and from $590 for 4 bedrooms or more. Call or email us to book one.

Add-ons and extras

  • Extra tasks, such as inside the oven, inside the fridge or interior windows, are billed at about $60 per cleaner hour, quoted before we start
  • Pets: $15 per visit, for the extra time on hair and floors. HEPA-filtered vacuums trap pet hair and dander on every visit
  • Heavily soiled homes may need a reset visit first

Cancellations and lockouts

  • Cancel or reschedule free with 24 hours notice
  • Less than 24 hours notice: a flat $40
  • If we cannot get in when your cleaner arrives: 50% of the visit

Our guarantee

If something was missed, tell us within 48 hours and we will send someone back to fix it at no charge. We would rather hear about it than lose you quietly.
