Regular clean, deep clean or move out clean?

What each one covers and which one you need.

Regular clean

The routine upkeep of a home that is already in decent shape: kitchen, bathrooms, dusting and floors. This is the base of our service, and the cheapest per visit.

Deep clean

A longer, more detailed clean that reaches things regular visits skip, such as baseboards, vents, cabinet fronts and grout. Companies often sell it as a separate service. We build it into the schedule instead.

How we handle it

  • Your first visit is a reset that covers the regular checklist plus the deeper items, priced higher than a regular visit because it takes longer.
  • After that, one area of your home gets extra attention on a rotation each visit, so the house stays deep clean without a big bill.

Move out and move in cleans

Empty homes need a full reset plus the inside of the fridge, oven and cabinets, so a move clean is the first visit price plus those three: $367.50 for a studio or 1 bedroom, $427.50 for 2 bedrooms, $517.50 for 3 bedrooms, and from $645 for 4 or more. Call or text us with the size of the home and your move date.

Which one do you need?

  • Ongoing upkeep: a regular schedule, starting with a reset
  • Guests coming or a fresh start: a one time reset
  • Moving: a move out or move in clean
